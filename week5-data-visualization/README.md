# Week 5: Data Visualization

This week's task was to create meaningful visualizations from the bank customer
dataset and explain what each one shows.

## What I Did

Built eight visualizations, each using a chart type suited to the question being asked:

1. Churn distribution (pie chart)
2. Customer age distribution (histogram)
3. Churn rate by geography (bar chart)
4. Account balance by churn status (box plot)
5. Correlation heatmap of numeric features
6. Churn rate trend by age group (line chart)
7. Churn by number of products held (stacked bar chart)
8. Credit score distribution by churn status (overlapping histograms)

## Key Findings

- About 20.4% of customers churned overall
- Germany churns at roughly double the rate of France and Spain
- Churn peaks sharply in the 50-60 age group
- Customers who churn have higher median balances ($109,344 vs. $92,063)
- New pattern: customers with 2 products churn the least, while those with 3 or 4
  products churn at very high rates
- Credit score shows no meaningful difference between churned and retained customers
