# Week 3: Risk Analysis

This week's task was to identify customer attrition risks using the Week 1 dataset,
assess their likelihood and financial impact, and propose mitigation strategies.

## What I Did

- Identified three key risks based on Week 1 findings: geographic concentration
  risk (Germany), age-related attrition risk (ages 50–60), and high-value customer
  attrition risk (balance-holders)
- Quantified each risk using both churn rate (likelihood) and total account
  balance lost to churn (financial impact)
- Built two charts comparing churn rate and dollar impact across the three risks
- Proposed specific mitigation strategies for each risk

## Key Result

Combined, the three risks are tied to over $330 million in account balances lost
to churn. High-value customer attrition carries the largest financial exposure
(~$185.68 million), despite having the lowest churn rate (24.1%) of the three —
showing that likelihood and dollar impact don't always align.
